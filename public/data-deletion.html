User Data Deletion Instructions

Dis·Insta Application

Last updated: September 2026

In accordance with Meta Platform Rules, Dis·Insta provides clear, automated, and manual options for users to request the permanent deletion of their account data.

Method 1: Instant In-App Data Disconnect

Instant Session & Data Clearing:

  1. Open Dis·Insta and switch to your Profile tab.
  2. Scroll down to the Settings section.
  3. Tap Disconnect Account.

This immediately destroys your authentication tokens, cleans active sessions, and wipes temporary message caches from our servers.

Method 2: Revoke Access via Instagram (Meta)

Revoke App Access through Instagram:

  1. Open your Instagram Mobile App or visit Instagram Manage Access.
  2. Go to Settings and privacy → Website permissions (or Apps and Websites).
  3. Find Dis·Insta in the Active list and tap Remove.

Once removed, Meta automatically invalidates all access tokens, cutting off all future API communication.

Method 3: Direct Data Deletion Request (Support)

If you would like our team to manually purge any lingering identifiers, backup logs, or records associated with your Instagram User ID, please send an email with your Instagram handle to:

Email: support@dis-insta.app
Subject: Data Deletion Request — [Your Instagram Handle]

Requests are processed within 48 hours, and a confirmation email will be returned once all records are purged.
