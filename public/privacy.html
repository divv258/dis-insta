Privacy Policy

Dis·Insta Application

Last updated: September 2026

Dis·Insta ("we", "our", or "us") provides a distraction-free direct messaging client for Instagram users. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in strict compliance with Meta Developer Policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and applicable privacy laws.

1. Information We Collect

When you authenticate via Instagram OAuth, we access only the data necessary to provide messaging functionality:

  • Profile Information: Your Instagram User ID, username, profile picture URL, name, follower count, and bio.
  • Direct Messages: Thread IDs, message content, timestamps, and participant IDs required to display and send your conversations.
  • Session Data: Encrypted authentication tokens stored in your secure browser session cookie.

We never collect or store your Instagram password. Authentication is handled directly by Instagram's secure OAuth servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Display your active Instagram direct message threads and contacts.
  • Deliver and receive messages via the official Meta Instagram Graph API.
  • Maintain your authenticated session while you use the application.

We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, analytics tracking, profiling, or machine learning model training.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third parties. Your data is only exchanged with Meta's official Graph API servers (graph.instagram.com) to provide the messaging service as authorized by you.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your session tokens and conversation caches are stored securely with industry-standard encryption. You can disconnect your account at any time by clicking "Disconnect Account" in your profile settings, which immediately destroys your local session.

5. Data Deletion (Your Rights)

You have the right to request deletion of all data associated with your Instagram account at any time. For step-by-step instructions or automated deletion requests, visit our User Data Deletion Page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at: support@dis-insta.app.
